Topline Overview of Use Cases

Foundational Themes
Mobile First: Ensure the app is optimized for mobile devices to provide a seamless and user-friendly experience.

Simplicity: Focus on intuitive design and easy navigation for all users.

Two User Personas: Cater to:

Individuals seeking personalized meal plans.
Fitness/Nutrition Coaches managing clients’ nutrition and fitness needs.

Habit Formation and Rewards: Encourage consistent usage through habit-forming tools and reward systems.

Highly Niche and Customizable: Support specific needs such as diabetic-friendly plans, professional sports nutrition, and other specialized diets.

Community Support: Foster engagement through group interactions, gamification, and shared goals.

Data-Driven Approach: Leverage analytics and user feedback to enhance recommendations and results.

Cultural Diversity: Incorporate meal plans and recipes inspired by global cuisines to cater to diverse preferences.

Education and Engagement: Provide users with insights and educational content about nutrition and healthy habits.









Use Cases for Individuals

AI-Based Recommendations: Generate personalized meal plans using input data such as age, height, allergens, and dietary preferences.

Community Building: Allow users to connect with others who have similar goals or interests and form support groups.

Habit Formation: Enable users to log activities and earn reward points for consistency and achievements.

Recipes and Meal Kits: Offer access to a library of recipes with an option to order meal kits directly.

Dynamic Adjustments: Provide meal plans that adapt based on user feedback, health data, or changing goals.

Wearable Integration: Sync with fitness wearables to adjust meal plans according to health metrics like activity levels or calorie expenditure.


Use Cases for Coaches

Client Meal Plans: Create personalized meal plans for clients based on their unique needs and preferences.

Client Management Dashboard: Use a centralized dashboard to monitor clients, analyze trends, and evaluate outcomes.

Favorite and Share Plans: Save successful meal plans and share them with other coaches for collaboration or inspiration.

Secure Communication: Offer a secure platform for messaging and exchanging feedback with clients.

Client Reports: Generate detailed reports to track client progress and demonstrate results.



Monetization

For Individuals

Basic Plan: Access a limited number of meal plans for a predefined duration (X meal plans for Y days).

Premium Subscription: Unlock all features, including habit-building tools and dynamic meal plans, through a monthly subscription.

Affiliate Partnerships: Partner with meal kit providers, recipe platforms, and grocery services to generate affiliate revenue.

For Coaches

Tiered Plans: Offer subscriptions based on the number of clients a coach manages.

White Labeling: Allow coaches to customize the app with their branding for an additional fee.

Sharing Features: Enable coaches to share plans with peers or clients for a premium cost.
